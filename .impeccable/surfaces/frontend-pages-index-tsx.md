# Job Pilot (all pages)

Mode: Operate. One person (freelance data/BI analyst) reviewing job matches on a laptop in the evening.
Task: find a job worth applying to, tailor the kit, apply, track. Standard web controls throughout.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The mechanism is tailoring: every job is judged by fit and the kit is cut from the master resume to that fit. The surface owns the *fit sheet* (what the posting asks beside what you have) and the *path* (review → tailor → apply, the active step carrying its own buttons). It refuses the category default of an inbox with tabs where the next action has to be hunted for.

OWN-WORLD: Warm charcoal ground with a one-step raised panel; hairline rules; off-white text; thread gold as the single accent for fit bars and the primary action; green/amber/red only as state. System sans for UI, a serif only where a document is shown. No gradients, glow, shadows or badges.

STORY: The visitor sees within seconds which jobs fit and why, trusts the kit (cut from their own resume, gaps shown), and does the next thing the path points at.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (wordmark, Jobs, Profile). Row of stage filters with counts; Find jobs at right with the last scan's result. Left: ranked list (score, title, company, source, age). Right: the selected job: score and title, the three-cell path with the active step raised, then the fit sheet.

FORM: Two-pane list + detail with stages as filters (tracker folded in), the path strip as the signature, the first-run setup walk in four steps, inline next-step copy on every state. Motion: fit bars fill on selection.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, and DESIGN.md.
